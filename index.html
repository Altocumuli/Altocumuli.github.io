Research focus

Agents that learn, search, and act with confidence.

I am interested in the methods and system design needed for agents to preserve context, learn from interaction, and operate reliably over long horizons.

01

LLM Agents

Designing agents that can reason, use tools, preserve evidence, and complete long-horizon tasks beyond a single model response.

02

Post-training & Agentic RL

Studying data, exploration, policy optimization, and evaluation methods that turn tool-use trajectories into reliable learning signals.

03

Diffusion Models

Exploring diffusion-based generative modeling and the broader principles behind controllable, high-quality generation.

About

Research-minded.
Systems-ready.

I am Altocumuli (杨憬晗), a third-year Software Engineering undergraduate at Tsinghua University. I work on LLM agents, agentic reinforcement learning, and the harnesses that help long-running agents remain reliable outside a notebook.

I am drawn to agents because they give language models the ability to interact with and affect the real world. My recent work at Kuaishou connects algorithmic research with system questions such as context growth, evidence consistency, state recovery, safety checks, and feedback-driven iteration. I value independent thinking, adapt quickly to new environments, and am comfortable working through uncertain, high-pressure problems.
